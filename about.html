Our Mission & Story

Bridging Human Connections with Pure Voice & Trust

In an era of superficial swiping and endless text screens, Hello24 brings back the warmth, spontaneity, and authenticity of real human voice conversations.

1. Social Friendship

Talk freely with friendly people (Boy/Girl match, casual banter, late night stories, language learning) without risking your personal phone number.

2. Professional Advice

Connect instantly with verified doctors, mental health counselors, astrologers, lawyers and tech mentors for private 1-on-1 consultations.

3. LiveKit WebRTC Core

Built with enterprise-grade LiveKit SFU and LiveKit Egress server architecture for crystal-clear Opus 48kHz HD sound and sub-40ms latency.

Our Strict Privacy Guarantee

At Hello24, your security and anonymity are non-negotiable. All calls are initiated over encrypted WebRTC channels. We never store personal contact numbers, never share customer phone numbers with hosts, and employ automated safety filters to protect our community.
