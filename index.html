Built on High-Performance LiveKit SFU & Egress

Enterprise WebRTC server technology delivering sub-40ms global latency, Opus HD audio encoding, and automated cloud recording via LiveKit Egress.

LiveKit SFU (Selective Forwarding Unit)

Direct WebRTC peer-to-server routing capable of millions of concurrent tracks with dynamically adaptive bitrates and sub-40ms latency.

LiveKit Egress Server (Recording & Streaming)

Containerized Egress workers render composite room audio/video, encode H.264/AAC, and upload encrypted recordings directly to AWS S3 / Google Cloud Storage for compliance.

100% App-to-App VoIP Privacy

Users never exchange real phone numbers or WhatsApp credentials. All communication happens purely through encrypted WebRTC tokens and ephemeral session keys.

Real-time Billing Webhook Dispatcher

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my phone number visible to the person I'm calling?
No, never! Hello24 is a 100% app-to-app VoIP platform built on WebRTC and LiveKit. Neither your phone number, real name, nor personal contacts are ever shared with any host or caller.
How does per-second billing and wallet work?
You recharge your Hello24 in-app wallet with secure UPI/Card/NetBanking. When you make a call, talktime is calculated strictly per-second. If a call lasts 2 minutes and 15 seconds, you only pay for exactly 135 seconds.
How can I consult a verified doctor or astrologer?
Simply browse the "Verified Professionals" tab, review their credentials, user ratings, and per-minute consultation fee. Tap "Consult Now" to start an instant private 1-on-1 audio or video session.
What technology powers the call audio quality?
Hello24 is powered by LiveKit SFU (WebRTC), utilizing Opus 48kHz high-definition audio compression, adaptive jitter buffers, and LiveKit Egress for server-side recording and compliance pipelines.
