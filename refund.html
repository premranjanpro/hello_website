Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Unused Wallet Balance Refund

Users may request a full refund of any unspent wallet balance directly back to their source bank account or original UPI ID within 14 days of recharge, with zero cancellation penalties.

2. Call Quality & Technical Dispute Claims

In the rare event of a call drop or network glitch caused by server-side LiveKit connectivity, users can report the call timestamp within 24 hours. Once verified, the exact debited talktime is instantly credited back to your wallet.

3. Host Cancellation Policy

If a scheduled consultation with a verified professional is cancelled prior to call initiation, 100% of the booking balance is restored immediately.
